# Problem Statement
# AtliQ Motors — India EV Market Intelligence & Expansion Strategy

---

## 1. Business Background

AtliQ Motors is a global automotive company specializing in electric and hybrid vehicles.

Over the last five years, the company has successfully established a strong position in the North American electric and hybrid vehicle market, reaching approximately **25% market share**.

As part of its international expansion strategy, AtliQ Motors is evaluating the Indian market.

India represents a potentially significant opportunity because of:

- Increasing electric vehicle adoption
- Growing environmental awareness
- Government support for electric mobility
- Increasing charging infrastructure
- Rising fuel costs
- Expansion of EV manufacturers
- Increasing consumer interest in electric vehicles
- Long-term growth potential of the Indian automobile market

However, AtliQ Motors currently has **less than 2% market share in India**.

The company therefore needs to determine whether, where, and how it should expand its EV business in India.

---

# 2. The Business Situation

AtliQ Motors cannot simply enter the Indian market by assuming that overall EV demand is sufficient.

India is not a single homogeneous market.

Each state has different:

- EV adoption levels
- Sales volumes
- Growth rates
- Consumer behavior
- Government incentives
- Charging infrastructure
- Competitive intensity
- Economic conditions
- Manufacturing ecosystem
- Market maturity

For example, a state may currently have high EV sales but relatively slow future growth.

Another state may have lower current sales but extremely high growth potential.

Similarly, a state may have strong EV penetration but intense competition, while another may have lower competition and significant untapped demand.

Therefore, **current sales alone cannot determine the best market for AtliQ Motors.**

The company needs a comprehensive data-driven approach that evaluates both the **current market situation and future opportunity**.

---

# 3. The Core Business Problem

The central problem facing AtliQ Motors is:

> **How can AtliQ Motors identify the most attractive opportunities within India's EV market and develop a data-driven strategy for market entry, product prioritization, regional expansion, manufacturing investment, and long-term growth?**
