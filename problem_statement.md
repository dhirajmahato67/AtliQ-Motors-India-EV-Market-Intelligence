# ⚡ AtliQ Motors — India EV Market Intelligence

> An end-to-end Microsoft Fabric analytics solution that transforms India's EV market data into actionable intelligence for AtliQ Motors' market-entry, expansion, manufacturing, product, and investment decisions.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

AtliQ Motors is a leading automotive company headquartered in the USA. Over the past five years, the company has established a strong position in the electric and hybrid vehicle market in North America, reaching approximately **25% market share**.

As part of its global expansion strategy, AtliQ Motors is evaluating the opportunity to enter the rapidly evolving **Indian EV market**, where its current market share is below **2%**.

Although India represents a significant long-term opportunity, entering the market without understanding its regional and competitive dynamics could expose the company to substantial investment risk.

The Indian EV market is highly diverse. EV adoption varies significantly across states due to differences in:

- EV penetration
- Consumer demand
- Vehicle affordability
- Government incentives and subsidies
- Charging infrastructure
- Manufacturer competition
- State-level policies
- Manufacturing ecosystem
- Historical growth
- Future market potential

Therefore, AtliQ Motors cannot rely only on national-level EV sales to make its expansion decisions.

The company requires a **data-driven market intelligence platform** capable of transforming raw automotive data into reliable, actionable insights for strategic decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Core Business Problem

The key question for AtliQ Motors leadership is:

> **Should AtliQ Motors expand into the Indian EV market, and if so, which states, vehicle segments, manufacturers, and strategic investments should the company prioritize?**

To answer this question, the business needs to understand:

### Market Size & Growth

- How is India's EV market evolving?
- What are the historical EV sales trends?
- Which vehicle categories are driving adoption?
- How rapidly is EV penetration increasing?
- What could the EV market look like by 2030?

### Competitive Landscape

- Which EV manufacturers currently dominate the market?
- Which manufacturers are growing the fastest?
- Which manufacturers are losing momentum?
- Where are the competitive opportunities for AtliQ Motors?

### Geographic Opportunity

- Which states have the highest EV penetration?
- Which states have the highest sales volume?
- Which states are growing the fastest?
- Which states are experiencing declining penetration?
- Which states have the strongest long-term potential?

### Customer & Adoption Factors

- Why are customers adopting EVs?
- How important are cost savings and environmental concerns?
- How do government incentives influence adoption?
- How does charging infrastructure affect EV penetration?

### Investment Strategy

- Where should AtliQ Motors establish manufacturing operations?
- Which states should be prioritized for market entry?
- Which vehicle segments should be targeted?
- Where should marketing investments be concentrated?
- How should AtliQ balance current market size with future growth?
